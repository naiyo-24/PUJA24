# PUJA24 - Application Features

This document outlines all the current features and modules implemented in the **PUJA24** application.

## 1. Authentication & Onboarding
- **Splash Screen:** Initial app loading screen.
- **Login Screen:** Phone number based login.
- **OTP Verification:** SMS verification for secure login.
- **Profile Creation:** Set up user profile (name, photo, etc.) after first login.
- **Onboarding:** Welcome flows to introduce the app.

## 2. Home & Explore
- **Home Screen / Explore:** The central dashboard featuring quick actions, categories, and highlights.
- **Categories:** Quick access to Pandals, Food & Cafes, Metro, Plan, Parking, Toilets, and Events.

## 3. Pandals (Puja Directory)
- **Puja Directory:** A comprehensive list of Durga Puja pandals.
- **Puja Details:** In-depth information, photos, and details for a specific pandal.
- **Puja Map:** An interactive map view to discover pandals geographically.

## 4. Transport & Navigation (Kolkata Metro)
- **Metro Guide:** A dedicated hub to view all Metro lines (Blue, Green, Purple, Orange).
- **Smart Route Planner:** Search for a destination (using Google Places API Autocomplete) to instantly find the nearest metro station to you, and the nearest to your destination.
- **Live Metro Map:** A full-screen interactive Google Map pinning all the operational Kolkata Metro stations.
- **Map Picker:** Interface to manually select custom locations on a map.

## 5. Food & Cafés
- **Cafe Directory:** A curated list of cafes and eateries around the city.
- **Restaurant Details:** Specific menus, timings, and information for restaurants.

## 6. Puja Pass (Ticketing & Payments)
- **Puja Pass Details:** Information on VIP passes or special access passes.
- **Pass Purchase Form:** Interface for users to fill in details and buy passes.
- **Payment Integration:** Secure checkout flow (e.g., Razorpay integration).
- **Payment Success:** Confirmation screen after a successful purchase.
- **My Passes:** A digital wallet for users to view and manage their purchased tickets.

## 7. Planning & Saved Places
- **Planner:** A smart scheduling tool to help users plan their pandal-hopping route.
- **Saved Places:** A bookmark feature allowing users to save their favorite pandals, cafes, or events for quick access.

## 8. User Profile & Settings
- **Profile Screen:** Manage user account details and preferences.
- **About Us:** Information about the PUJA24 team and mission.
- **Legal:** Privacy Policy and Terms of Service screens.

## 9. Additional Features (Under Development)
- **Events:** Directory for cultural events, competitions, and shows during the festival.
- **Reviews:** User ratings and feedback for pandals and restaurants.
- **Notifications:** Push alerts for important updates, crowd levels, or ticketing.
- **Search:** Global search functionality across the entire app.
